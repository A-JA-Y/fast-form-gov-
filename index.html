03 — Anatomy of a compliant photo

What the portal actually checks.

Most rejections come down to four things: wrong pixel size, file over the KB limit, a busy background, or a face that is too small in the frame. The diagram measures them out as you scroll.

  • Exact pixels. Portals validate width × height, not the aspect ratio. GovForms exports at the precise size.
  • Under the ceiling. A binary search over JPEG quality lands the file just under your KB limit.
  • Plain background. Choose white or light blue fill for any letter-boxed area.
  • Face fills 70–80%. Zoom in the editor until the head sits inside the guide.

        12 — FAQ

        Questions people ask at 11:58 pm.

        Are my files uploaded anywhere?
        No. Every crop, compression and merge runs in your browser using the Canvas API. The page loads a few libraries from public CDNs, but your files never leave this device.
        Which dimensions should I use?
        Templates carry the values most commonly quoted for each exam, but portals do change them. Check the official notification and use the Custom template if yours differs. Cards marked “verify” use generic passport-size defaults.
        My PNG is bigger than the limit. Why?
        PNG is lossless, so its size cannot be tuned much. Switch the output format to JPEG and set a Max size in KB, and GovForms will find the highest quality that fits.
        How do I get a clean signature from a phone photo?
        Sign on plain white paper, photograph it in good light, and keep the “Remove paper background” toggle on. GovForms lifts the paper to pure white and deepens the ink.
        What about thumb impressions and handwritten declarations?
        Bank exams such as IBPS, SBI and RBI ask for them. Pick that template and the extra upload slots appear automatically with the right sizes and KB limits.
        Can I install GovForms on my phone?
        Yes. On Android and desktop Chrome, tap the install icon in the top bar. On iPhone, open the Share menu in Safari and choose “Add to Home Screen”. Once installed it works offline.
        Where does the news come from?
        Headlines are pulled daily from Google News (India edition) using searches for each exam category, then de-duplicated. Every story links to its original publisher. Always confirm dates on the official notice board.
